About Dashink

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Dashink is a fast arcade game where you draw ink lines to guide a rolling ball. A Fly Work product. No ads, no sign-up — just you, the ball and the ink.

1. How to play

  • Draw ink with your finger; the ball rolls along the path you create.
  • Go as far as you can, avoid obstacles and chase a high score.
  • Try the daily challenge, complete tasks and check the leaderboards.
  • Spend coins in the shop on balls, ink colours and themes.

2. Purchases

The game is free. Optional Dashink Premium (one-time) is purchased through the App Store and Google Play. Details: Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
